# Amoxtli · Sourced excerpts, expansion 02 (October 27, 2026)

**Four short reading excerpts** have been added to Amoxtli. They link directly to the excerpt/edition, preserve original spelling, and provide deliberately editorial normalizations and translations. They include descriptive fragments as well as poetic material; none is falsely presented as a self-standing complete sentence if it is only a clause fragment.

## Reading sources

1. **Fish in water**: `atl yitic nemjnj`. Sahagún, *Florentine Codex*, Book 11, folio 62r, as presented in the Getty Digital Florentine Codex, Anderson–Dibble transcription. Normalized `ātl yitic nemini`. The noun/verb distinction for *nemini* is ambiguous in isolation.
2. **Comparison**: `iuhqujn chimalli`. Same folio, under chimalmichin. Normalized `iuhquin chimalli`. This is a simile fragment, not a finite sentence.
3. **Paint and flowers**: `injn itoca itech qujça xochitl, ioan tlapalli`. Sahagún, Book 11, folio 217r, Getty Digital Florentine Codex, Anderson–Dibble transcription. Normalized `inīn itoca ītech quiza xōchitl īhuān tlapalli`. *Itoca* is a possessed form of already-listed *tocaitl*, not a new lexical noun.
4. **Transience**: `cen tiyahui, tipolihui`. Cantares Mexicanos fol. 35r **as quoted in** Miguel León-Portilla, *La filosofía náhuatl estudiada en sus fuentes* (UNAM 2017), p. 408, excerpt 53. This is a secondary edited print, not independent manuscript inspection. The collective reading of `ti-` is contextual, and the translation is interpretive.
