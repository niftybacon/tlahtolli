# Stone fashioned into a mirror — Amoxtli note (2026-10-27)

## Original cited Nahuatl

**in mochioa tezcatl, in mocuepa ca tetl**

**Normalized editorial reading:** *in mochīhua tēzcatl in mocuepa ca tetl*

**Interpretive English:** “Stone from which a mirror is made; a stone transformed into a mirror.”

**Source:** Bernardino de Sahagún and Nahua collaborators, *Florentine Codex*, Book 11, folio 209v, heading of fifth section about stones used to make mirrors. Getty Digital Florentine Codex, Anderson–Dibble Nahuatl transcription.
https://florentinecodex.getty.edu/es/book/11/folio/209v

This is a **short original clause**, not an invented quotation about clairvoyance. The historical text speaks about mirror-making from stone. Cultures of Central Mexico also used polished mineral mirrors (obsidian and pyrite among other materials) in ritual and divination. That wider association with reflection and visionary insight is supported by the [LACMA Mirror exhibit](https://www.lacma.org/guides/mixpantli/02) but must not be presented as what this specific sentence literally states.

## Lexical indexing and limits

- *tetl* — **stone**, pre-existing headword. Inanimate noun; no verified lexical plural in existing entry.
- *tēzcatl* — **mirror / polished reflective stone**, Molina (1571) II fol. 112v col. 2, Karttunen (1992) p. 239, [Online Nahuatl Dictionary](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/tezcatl). Original folio has *tezcatl*. The source-reported *tezcame* plural is cited from Florentine Codex Book 6 through the dictionary's scholarly transcription; this does not imply all dialects share the form.
- *chīhua* — **make**, already a headword. Manuscript *mochioa* is provisionally lemmatized to it rather than added as a new noun/verb.
- *cuepa* — **turn / return / transform**; Molina (1571) II f. 26r via [ODN](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/cuepa). Manuscript reflexive/nonactive *mocuepa* is indexed as a separately cited form; no unverified full paradigm.
- *ca* — **explanatory/emphatic particle** (Molina 1571, f. 10r) with a distinct existential verb homograph; [ODN particle](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/node/175904) and [ODN verb](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/ca). The occurrence in this passage is **grammatically ambiguous** and its exact function requires specialist review. The dictionary's citation form does not silently conflate the two readings.
- *in* — pre-existing grammatical particle.

The transliterated passage is short to comply with the rights restrictions of the Anderson–Dibble edited transcription. The manuscript/folio and distinct lexicographic sources are openly linked. Historical transcription ≠ independently checked manuscript image, and the English interpretation is not a claim of verbatim translation.
