**October 29, 2026. Source boundary:** Before this review, the canonical dictionary held 210 words: 130 source-checked, 80 index-only. The table below covers **every one of those exact 80 records** in source order, including nonverbal entries. Review is scoped to **whether the recorded material supports a verb class**; an index noun/adverb is *not* being pronounced independently verified merely because it was counted here.

## Outcome (no artificial grammar coverage)

- **14** index-only candidate verbs promoted to entry-level source-checked lexical classes, with **10** exact Molina 1571 first-person preterites. Molina's `ni`, `nic`, `nite`, `nitla` labels are retained as **entry-specific** valency patterns, not a license for every prefix or future stem.
- **7** verb-like records remain deliberately unresolved. Source passages or lexicon glosses alone may not independently establish a productive stem or disambiguated lexical class.
- **59** current nonverbal index candidates (54 nouns, 2 relational/locative expressions, 2 adverbs, 1 adjective) were examined for **verb-class eligibility**, not independently upgraded as reviewed nouns or transformed into verbs. They remain index-only.
- **66** index-only records remain in the published 210-word canonical dictionary; **144** have entry-level checked lexical evidence. There are **267** stored form records, **1,272** field claims, **12** complete 10×6 grammar-derived paradigm matrices.

## Methodological safeguards

1. Source URLs above lead to Online Nahuatl Dictionary listings, its entry transcriptions of Molina 1571, or its Karttunen 1992 extracts. An **entry-level source check is not independent collation of an original Molina folio, a facsimile, or human-scholar approval**.
2. Original spellings such as `tecpillatoa`, `tecpancapoa`, `tecpancateneua` and `tepuzpitza` are preserved separately from normalized citation words. No arbitrary `u→o`, `a→hua`, accent/macron rewrite or allomorph is treated as a universal grammatical rule.
3. **All promoted `stem` claims remain unverified**. Exact 1sg examples are lexicographic readings; 2sg/present substitutions, where licensed by the existing canonical analyzer, remain `grade:hypothesis`. A source-reported `nite-` construction is admitted to the same analyzer with an exact-object-slot gate, not to a second parser.
4. `nonotza` is **not** automatically conflated with similar historical homographs; `tepozotinemi` and `tlamattinemi` retain specific historical leads for further person/stem adjudication.
5. No archive row, malformed OCR, Axolotl sample token, uncertain historical translation, or hypothetical generated surface was promoted into a reviewed headword or held-out gold. No change to the 36,543-row archive and no inferred translation accuracy gain.
6. This single PR deliberately does **not** modify concurrent draft PRs #64 or #65; rebase/reconcile those only after this scoped lexical review is merged and audited.
