# Editorial evidence policy — Tlahtōlli

Tlahtōlli is an independently maintained, **non-peer-reviewed** Classical Nahuatl research and learning project. A URL or successful schema check does **not** make a grammatical claim true.

## Evidence labels

- **Historical text:** a concrete form from an identifiable passage, with folio/page and source URL. A manuscript example does not imply universal usage.
- **Lexicographic entry:** the form is listed in a dictionary, sometimes with a named underlying source; do not call it independently manuscript-attested.
- **Published grammar / paradigm:** a pattern is documented in a grammar. Other forms derived from that pattern must remain marked grammar-derived.
- **Editorial inference:** an analytical reconstruction requiring further confirmation. Not equivalent to attestation.
- **Unverified:** a lead for future research, not accepted evidence.

## Rules

1. Distinguish dialect/era and the spelling that the source actually records.
2. Keep noun animacy separate from exceptional pluralization; do not generalize from mountain or star nouns.
3. Do not apply noun number or animacy labels to verbs and particles.
4. Cite claims separately (headword, part of speech, animacy, plural, possessed form, stem) with exact locations.
5. No source recorded means **unknown**, not grammatically impossible.
6. Do not generate or label reconstructed forms as historical attestations.
7. Record editorial corrections and conflicting readings rather than hiding them.
8. Never claim human scholar verification without genuine independent specialist review.
9. Preserve attribution and copyright; do not bulk-copy reference works or manuscript images.

## New-entry workflow

Research the original spelling, meaning, part of speech and variety. Enter source URL **and an exact source locator**. Add appropriate grammatical classifications and each attested form with its own evidence level. Check alternate analyses, run the tests, review changes, and publish approved additions through GitHub. The site then synchronizes published entries; user notes and learning progress remain local.
