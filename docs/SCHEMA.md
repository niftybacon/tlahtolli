# Tlahtōlli lexical schema
The published dictionary and verb paradigms have a matching release version. A compressed offline copy of the dictionary is embedded in `index.html`.

Each lemma records headword, English senses, historical variety, part of speech, source spelling, stem, noun animacy, number/plural behavior and individual source-backed inflected forms. `pluralStatus=no_attested_form` does **not** mean plural is impossible.

`inflections[]`: each recorded spelling has a grammatical category, English gloss, evidence type (dictionary/text/paradigm/stem/review), citation URL and notes. A grammar-generated form must not be called a manuscript attestation.

`claims[]`: source-specific assertions each include field, assessment (dictionary/text/grammar/inference/unverified), citation URL, exact location, explanatory note, optional researcher and review date. General lexical URLs are useful references but not independent validation of every field.

The 12 verb-paradigm matrices have ten rows of tense/mood patterns and six subject-person columns. These 720 cells are rule-derived examples, not 720 historical attestations. The morphological explorer aligns only already indexed forms.

Browser-local edits, manuscript notes, study progress and reader annotations are **not** public and are not synchronized between devices. Export a full backup to preserve them.
