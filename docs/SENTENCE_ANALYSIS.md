# Clause analysis: present-tense baseline

The sentence layer indexes all 72 present cells of the existing 12 grammar-derived paradigms, preserving person and transitivity. It accepts an indexed predicate alone, optionally preceded by ahmo/ahmō, and a third-person singular intransitive predicate with a contiguous `in + noun` subject before or after it. Every token must be consumed; unsupported material makes that clause incomplete. Periods, exclamation marks, semicolons and newlines delimit independently analyzed units; they are engineering boundaries, not a manuscript sentence segmentation claim. Questions are explicitly unsupported.

It returns structured frames (predicate, person, stated subject, object, polarity, tense), candidate sources and coverage. Transitive indexed cells preserve their fixed third-person object as him/her/it; no gender or antecedent is inferred. Stated noun subjects are restricted to the 3sg analysis; plural noun agreement, transitive noun-phrase assignment, coordination, subordination and discourse reference are future work. Other tenses and homographic analyses may exist; a present candidate does not exclude them. Exact NFC/case-normalized cell matches are required; there is no diacritic folding in this layer.

English generation is deliberately separate from parsing. Three initial lexical renderers (chōca: cry/weep; tlacuā: eat; itta: see/inspect/look at) supply illustrative candidate sentences; other verbs expose the full indexed lexical meaning with their grammatical frame. These options are not exhaustive and do not constitute sense disambiguation. Noun meaning lists remain visible. English do-support is used in this initial renderer; it may sound emphatic. Present readings do not resolve simple/progressive aspect. A complete parse means coverage under this restricted grammar, not verified translation correctness.

Consulted 2026-10-28 in the publisher preview: https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780511985027_A23683606/preview-9780511985027_A23683606.pdf . The implementation is our bounded editorial formalization, not a claim that the book endorses its coverage.
- Existing dictionary.json and paradigms.json retain their provenance and evidence grades. No new paradigm cells or manuscript readings were introduced. Each candidate links its lexical/paradigm sources and grammar reference.

The automated controls cover all 72 present cells, both subject positions, person disagreement, explicit object preservation, clause-local negation, unsupported tokens, questions and input limits. These are constructed software controls, not held-out historical accuracy measurements. Browser checks verify that candidate translations and incomplete-clause explanations reach the interface. There has been no expert review or training.
