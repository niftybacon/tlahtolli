## Canonical-engine change: compositional *evidence*, not new conjugation

The phase 3 local `ayac tle` candidate remains independently source-backed by Bedell/Wimmer and is kept even when no finite data pack exists. The optional [exact two-shape source pack](../research/translation_corpus/source_graded_itoa_speech_morphology_2026_10_30.json) is fetched by the **existing** `translator-integrated.js` reader and passed as the *last optional argument* of `TlahtolliSentence.create`. It must pass a **whole-pack fail-closed gate** on the two exact written forms, proof sources and all non-promotion flags.

When an original input has three adjacent tokens **`ayac tle quitoz`** or **`ayac tle oquito`**, the **existing sentence engine** adds one `finiteSpeechCandidate` *alongside*, not replacing, the local negative-indefinite pair. Each candidate stores the original written token, exact source-indexed form, grammar-compatible shape (future `-z` vs preterite `o-`), original token index, lexical and grammatical references, document IDs, and explicit `false` values for resolved tense, person, argument roles, reference, polarity scope, verified segmentation, and English. **It creates no general `itoa` conjugation or freely substitutable forms.**

`translator-grammar-ir.js` **independently verifies every original written token, indexed documentary form, sources, grammar link and unresolved flag**, then adds a `source-graded-itoa-speech-shape` node and an explicitly noncertifying edge to the existing negative-indefinite candidate. It does *not* create a complete finite-predicate parse. `translator-english.js` keeps both historical-witness and local negative-indefinite English refusals. The existing historical diagnostic reports `sourceGradedSpeechFiniteShapes` and `grammarIRSpeechFiniteShapes` **separately** from `grammaticalNegativeIndefinitePairs`, complete clauses and lexical recognition. The public translator cites the finite grammar evidence and preserves the written form.

## Follow-up: source-graded speech content and attested 1sg comparison

The [ODN `tle(h)` entry](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/tle) cites Karttunen (1992, p.307) for the **non-interrogative negative sense** of `tle(h)` under negation; it does not make every written `tle` mean “nothing,” nor license global `tlen` normalization. [Wimmer's `ayâc` entry on UNAM GDN](https://gdn.iib.unam.mx/diccionario/ayac/41417) quotes Sahagún Book XII, chapter 6, **`ayâc tle quihtoa`**, a useful independently indexed *comparative construction*. Those two sources support a **possible negative speech-content constituent** for original written `tle` in exact local `ayac tle quitoz` / `ayac tle oquito`, not a certified direct object, exhaustive negation scope, or a reviewed sentence English. The latter are still unresolved in each edited legal witness.

A distinct primary lexicon citation improves the past-form comparison: [Alonso de Molina 1571, II folio 43r, column 2, transcribed by ODN](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/content/itoa), explicitly gives **`itoa. niqu. (pret. oniquito.)`** for saying something. This is a *source-attested first-person completed-form comparison* for the original `oquito`, **not** authority to delete `ni-` to create new attested words, infer the legal document's subject person, erase spelling differences, or decide past versus present perfect in that context. Future-form `quitoz` **must not inherit** this past-comparison annotation.

The two-form source pack now contains (a) the exact `tle` lexical and Sahagún grammar references and explicit uncertain-role flags, and (b) Molina's actual `oniquito` evidence with no person-template permission. **The existing** sentence analyzer includes the possible content-argument and completed-form comparative evidence *only* when the exact source pack, original `ayac tle`, and the matching written `quitoz`/`oquito` are all present. **The existing shared grammar IR independently cross-checks** source URLs, written token indices, scholarly comparison, and *all* false role/English flags; it adds `source-graded-tle-content-candidate` as a *candidate attachment* to the written finite-shape node and, **only for `oquito`**, `source-graded-molina-1sg-comparator`. This is a syntactic research candidate **inside the actual canonical graph**, not a full finite predicate, productive lexical entry, or human-approved English realization.

The live source panel now displays **both independent citations**, preserves the exact original `tle` and `oquito` spelling, and states the unresolved argument/person analysis. The actual historical diagnostic separately counts content-attachment and Molina-comparison evidence nodes, without affecting original lexical-route coverage. Extended `tools/test_itoa_speech_morphology.js` requires one content candidate for each of five public historical contexts and the Molina comparator **only** in the three completed-form contexts. It includes forged document/lexical sources, object certainty, negative scope, claim status, English, third-token substitution, future/past leakage and past/perfect fabricated resolution. Existing `nichōca` finite English stays accepted.

**Reproducible effect to claim:** one new conditional *possible* syntactic content relationship plus one attested external first-person **comparison**, not two reviewed historical syntactic parses, no actual complete-clause English and no newly generated finite forms. The independent **926 unmatched / 1,181** lexical-route baseline and **2,144** headwords remain unchanged; approved Silver/Gold remain **zero**.
