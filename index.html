Tlāhcuīlōlli · Evidence first

Research and verification audit

Evidence completeness is not proof of linguistic correctness. A general dictionary link does not verify every grammatical field. These checks identify where human review is still needed.

Use the Evidence tab to cite a particular grammatical claim with a page, folio, section, or dictionary entry. No claims here are independently peer-reviewed.

1 · Dictionary

Document a word

Add meanings, explicit number and animate/inanimate classifications, attested plurals, original spellings and citations. Every entry has Forms / Inflections and Evidence tabs for documented forms and claim-specific sourcing. The Research audit tab prioritizes missing evidence. Blank grammar fields mean unknown—not automatically inanimate or singular. A draft word is not historically verified. Keep different regional varieties distinct.

2 · Reader

Read a text

Add an original manuscript passage and a carefully checked normalized transcription. Click words to look up, edit, and annotate their forms without leaving the reader. Exact headwords and listed historical spellings are matched automatically; the reader shows indexed-coverage counts and an exportable list of unknown tokens; manually linked words remain suggested analyses until reviewed. Source-linked forms can match tokens automatically, but ambiguous grammatical analyses require manual checking. The new Interlinear annotation panel can export per-token drafts with evidence, sources and unresolved readings; it is not a scholarly morphological corpus.

3 · Anki & safety

Learn and back up

Anki handles repetition; export cards only if useful. Back up dictionary plus passages as a JSON file regularly. All your edits remain local to this browser unless exported.

A manuscript's transcription, vowel lengths, and translation should be attributed to a source. Do not present an automatically matched form as proof of historical morphology, and do not publish sample entries as verified research.

Dictionary includes source-checked grammatical labels; unsourced fields remain visibly unverified.
