# Send a source to Tlahtōlli

**One source is enough to start.** Copy this outline into a message, filling in anything you know. Unknown details are fine.

- **Original text source:** (URL or title)
- **Where in the source:** (book, chapter, folio, page, line, timestamp, or passage title)
- **Translation source:** (URL, edition, or “none found”)
- **Language of translation:** (English / Spanish / other / unknown)
- **Nahuatl variety:** (Classical / Eastern Huasteca / other / unknown)
- **Audio recording, if any:** (URL, speaker/reader if credited, and timestamp)
- **Permission or licensing information:** (license URL / permission received / unknown)
- **Why this is useful or any uncertainties:** (optional)

It's fine to send just the two links in chat. The research team will investigate alignment, manuscript context, copyright, and accuracy before creating any approved training records.

For speech data, **do not upload someone else's recordings or imitate a person's voice for a synthetic voice without appropriate permission and a clear legitimate use.** Text/translation corpus intake comes first.
